Kitchen Hot Water

Food-Service Hot Water Systems Built for the Rush

In a restaurant, the dish machine, the sinks, and the handwashing stations all pull from the same supply, and an undersized or failing heater shows up first as a dish machine that can't hit temperature and stacks of dirty plates. A high-temperature dish machine usually needs final rinse water near 180°F, which almost always calls for a booster heater working alongside the main system. Our food-service work covers high-input tanks, tankless racks, booster heaters, and sizing for peak demand, and we schedule every visit around your prep and service times.

Temperature is where many restaurant hot water problems start. When the main heater falls behind, handwashing sinks run lukewarm, the three-compartment sink falls below temperature, and the dish machine struggles to sanitize during the rush. Our technicians verify temperatures from the tank all the way to the fixtures and document every visit in written reports your inspector and landlord can review.

We work around your business, not the other way around. Our team coordinates every replacement and maintenance visit with owners, managers, and kitchen staff. Off-hours installation is available when needed, and downtime stays short so the kitchen opens on time.

Safe Installation

Safe Installations, Documented Service

Commercial property owners have a duty of care to provide safe conditions for employees, customers, and tenants. A poorly installed or neglected water heater can lead to carbon monoxide hazards from bad venting, scalding at the tap, Legionella growth in water stored too cool, and costly water damage from a failed tank. Bloomington Water Heater provides commercial installations and inspections that cover venting and combustion air, gas line sizing, relief valve discharge piping, expansion tanks, thermostatic mixing valves, drain pans, seismic strapping where required, and permits and inspections. Our written reports identify risks, assign priority levels, and recommend specific preventive actions.

This work serves a dual purpose: it protects people and property, and it provides the documentation that demonstrates due diligence to landlords, insurers, and health or building inspectors. We maintain detailed service logs, temperature records, relief valve test results, and a documentation binder for every account, with more frequent service for heavy-use equipment in kitchens, laundries, and large mechanical rooms.

Bloomington Water Heater offers fast emergency water heater repair for commercial properties throughout the area, and we can schedule planned work after hours to keep downtime short.
